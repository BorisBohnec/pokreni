# PoKreni

A free Android app that teaches you to train safely and logs your gym sessions. It works alongside [Tanjur](https://github.com/BorisBohnec/tanjur): both apps share data through Health Connect, so Tanjur adds your workouts to its calorie budget and PoKreni shows the protein you logged in Tanjur.

English and Croatian; switch in Profile or with the EN/HR button at the top.

## What's inside

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `www/` | The whole app: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` (plain JS, no build step), bundled fonts |
| `www/logic.js` | Training logic with no UI: readiness check, double progression, pain rules, weekly totals, lesson checks |
| `www/i18n.js` | Interface text in English and Croatian |
| `www/data/exercises.js` | 39 exercises with cues, common mistakes, easier and harder versions, in both languages |
| `www/data/programs.js` | Foundations (weeks 1–4) and Build (weeks 5–12): full-body A/B sessions |
| `www/data/lessons.js` | The 19 lessons, in both languages |
| `www/ai.js` | AI client ported from Tanjur: Claude first while under the monthly limit, then free OpenRouter models, then Groq |
| `www/coach.js` | Builds the coach's prompt from your training, food and lessons, and turns its answer into safe HTML with lesson and exercise buttons |
| `www/img/ex/` | Exercise pictures from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (public domain), resized |
| `plugins/hc-bridge/` | Small native Health Connect add-on shared with Tanjur (see below) |
| `tests/` | `npm test`: progression, readiness, weekly totals, every text in both languages, and the coach's prompt, links, emergency check and AI fallbacks |
| `android/` | Native Android project generated by Capacitor 8 |
| `.github/workflows/build-apk.yml` | Builds the APK on GitHub on every push; publishes a release from `main` |

## Signing key

The key lives only in this repo's Actions secrets, never in the code:

- `POKRENI_KEYSTORE_B64`: the keystore file, base64 encoded
- `POKRENI_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`: its password (key alias `pokreni`)

Every release is signed with it, so updates install over the old app and keep your data. Keep a copy of the keystore somewhere safe: without it, a new APK can't update the installed one. Without the secrets the build still runs but produces an unsigned APK and no release.

## Get the APK onto your phone

1. Push to `main`. The Actions tab shows "Build PoKreni APK" (about 5 minutes).
2. On your phone open `https://github.com/BorisBohnec/pokreni/releases/latest` and download **pokreni.apk**.
3. Open it and tap **Install**. Updates install over the old version and keep your data.

Pushes to other branches build the APK too (download it from the run's artifacts) but don't publish a release.

## How it works with Tanjur

The two apps read each other directly, with no setup: each writes a small `share.json` into its private folder, and `plugins/hc-bridge` exposes it through a content provider that only Tanjur and PoKreni may read. PoKreni gets your weight, name, targets and today's meals from Tanjur this way; Tanjur gets your sessions and walks from PoKreni. Typing your weight into PoKreni is only needed when Tanjur has none.

Health Connect is optional on top of that:

| Direction | What |
| --- | --- |
| PoKreni → Health Connect | Each finished session (strength training) and each walk or ride, with estimated active calories |
| Health Connect → Tanjur | Tanjur already reads workouts and adds them to the day's budget |
| Tanjur → Health Connect | Weigh-ins and meal nutrition (kcal, protein, carbs, fat), when switched on in Tanjur |
| Health Connect → PoKreni | Weight, last night's sleep, steps, workouts from your watch, and protein from Tanjur |
| Deep links | `pokreni://today`, `pokreni://train`, `pokreni://cardio`, `pokreni://learn`; PoKreni opens `tanjur://today` and `tanjur://add` |

On Android, Capacitor plugins' `addListener()` returns a plain `{ remove }` object when `@capacitor/core` isn't bundled (it isn't here), so `app.js` attaches every native listener through `listen()`; a test checks this. Back closes the open sheet, then returns to Today, then leaves the app.

`@capgo/capacitor-health` reads most things but can't write exercise sessions and only writes nutrition as energy. `plugins/hc-bridge` is one Kotlin file that fills those gaps, using stable record IDs so editing or deleting something in either app updates Health Connect instead of duplicating it. The same folder is in the Tanjur repo.

## Ask the coach

The coach answers questions about training, pain, recovery and food, using your recent sessions, the plan, the lessons you've done and what Tanjur knows (weight, targets, recent meals). It answers in the language of your question and links to the matching lessons and exercises.

- **No keys of its own.** In Tanjur, Profile → AI, switch on *Let PoKreni use these AI keys*. Tanjur then passes its Claude, OpenRouter and Groq keys to PoKreni over the direct link. PoKreni keeps them in memory only, never in its data file or backups, and forgets them if you switch it off.
- **One Claude limit.** Both apps report what they spent this month, and Tanjur's monthly Claude limit covers the total. Once it's reached, the free models answer until next month.
- **Safety first.** Questions that sound like an emergency (chest pain, fainting, numbness, and the like, in English or Croatian) show the 112 card before any AI answer. The coach is told never to diagnose or suggest medicines, and to send you to a doctor when pain or symptoms call for it.
- **Your notes.** Profile → *For the coach* is a short free-text field (injuries, what your doctor said) that's added to every question.

Your question, the last few messages and that summary go to the AI service; nothing else leaves the phone.

## Build it on your PC instead

Needs Node 22+, JDK 21 and Android Studio.

```bash
npm install
npm test
npx cap sync android
npx cap open android
```

To try the app in a desktop browser, serve `www/` (for example `python -m http.server` inside it) and open `index.html`. Health Connect, notifications and opening Tanjur only work in the Android app.

## Safety

PoKreni gives general guidance for healthy beginners, not medical advice. The readiness check is based on the PAR-Q; the official, longer PAR-Q+ is at eparmedx.com.
