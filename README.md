# PoKreni

A free Android app that teaches you to train safely and logs your gym sessions. It works alongside [Tanjur](https://github.com/BorisBohnec/tanjur): both apps share data through Health Connect, so Tanjur adds your workouts to its calorie budget and PoKreni shows the protein you logged in Tanjur.

English and Croatian; switch in Profile or with the EN/HR button at the top.

## What's inside

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `www/` | The whole app: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` (plain JS, no build step), bundled fonts |
| `www/logic.js` | Training logic with no UI: readiness check, double progression, pain rules, weekly totals, lesson checks |
| `www/i18n.js` | Interface text in English and Croatian |
| `www/data/exercises.js` | 39 exercises with cues, common mistakes, easier and harder versions, in both languages |
| `www/data/programs.js` | Foundations (weeks 1–4) and Build (weeks 5–12): full-body A/B sessions, and the warm-up with a how-to for each move |
| `www/data/lessons.js` | The 19 lessons, in both languages |
| `www/data/body.js` | Body map: muscles, joints, the figure's shapes, and which muscles and joints each exercise loads |
| `www/anim.js`, `www/data/moves.js` | Exercise animations: a figure posed by joint angles (side or front view, with equipment), animated between key poses; works offline |
| `www/data/videos.js` | One YouTube demo clip per exercise, played inside the app |
| `www/ai.js` | AI client ported from Tanjur: Claude first while under the monthly limit, then free OpenRouter models, then Groq |
| `www/coach.js` | Builds the coach's prompt from your training, food and lessons, and turns its answer into safe HTML with lesson and exercise buttons |
| `www/img/ex/` | Exercise pictures from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (public domain), resized |
| `plugins/hc-bridge/` | Small native Health Connect add-on shared with Tanjur (see below) |
| `tests/` | `npm test`: progression, readiness, weekly totals, every text in both languages, the body map, ranks, injuries, workout builder, and the coach's prompt, cards, emergency check and AI fallbacks |
| `android/` | Native Android project generated by Capacitor 8 |
| `.github/workflows/build-apk.yml` | Builds the APK on GitHub on every push; publishes a release from `main` |

## Signing key

The key lives only in this repo's Actions secrets, never in the code:

- `POKRENI_KEYSTORE_B64`: the keystore file, base64 encoded
- `POKRENI_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`: its password (key alias `pokreni`)

Every release is signed with it, so updates install over the old app and keep your data. Keep a copy of the keystore somewhere safe: without it, a new APK can't update the installed one. Without the secrets the build still runs but produces an unsigned APK and no release.

## Get the APK onto your phone

1. Push to `main`. The Actions tab shows "Build PoKreni APK" (about 5 minutes).
2. On your phone open `https://github.com/BorisBohnec/pokreni/releases/latest` and download **pokreni.apk**.
3. Open it and tap **Install**. Updates install over the old version and keep your data.

Pushes to other branches build the APK too (download it from the run's artifacts) but don't publish a release.

## How it works with Tanjur

The two apps read each other directly, with no setup: each writes a small `share.json` into its private folder, and `plugins/hc-bridge` exposes it through a content provider that only Tanjur and PoKreni may read. PoKreni gets your weight, name, targets and today's meals from Tanjur this way; Tanjur gets your sessions and walks from PoKreni. Typing your weight into PoKreni is only needed when Tanjur has none.

Health Connect is optional on top of that:

| Direction | What |
| --- | --- |
| PoKreni → Health Connect | Each finished session (strength training) and each walk or ride, with estimated active calories |
| Health Connect → Tanjur | Tanjur already reads workouts and adds them to the day's budget |
| Tanjur → Health Connect | Weigh-ins and meal nutrition (kcal, protein, carbs, fat), when switched on in Tanjur |
| Health Connect → PoKreni | Weight, last night's sleep, steps, heart rate, workouts from your watch, and protein from Tanjur |
| Deep links | `pokreni://today`, `pokreni://train`, `pokreni://cardio`, `pokreni://learn`; PoKreni opens `tanjur://today` and `tanjur://add` |

On Android, Capacitor plugins' `addListener()` returns a plain `{ remove }` object when `@capacitor/core` isn't bundled (it isn't here), so `app.js` attaches every native listener through `listen()`; a test checks this. Back closes the open sheet, then returns to Today, then leaves the app.

`@capgo/capacitor-health` reads most things but can't write exercise sessions and only writes nutrition as energy. `plugins/hc-bridge` is one Kotlin file that fills those gaps, using stable record IDs so editing or deleting something in either app updates Health Connect instead of duplicating it. The same folder is in the Tanjur repo; PoKreni's copy also has `openApp` (opens Zepp or its Play Store page).

## Smartwatch (Amazfit Active 3 Premium and other Zepp watches)

Amazfit watches can't be paired with other apps: they only talk to the Zepp app. Zepp can share to Health Connect, and PoKreni reads it from there, so no extra login or API key is involved.

1. In Zepp: Profile → 3rd-party account linking → Health Connect → turn on *Sync with Health Connect*, then *Allow all*.
2. In PoKreni: Profile → Health Connect → connect (or *Ask again* if you connected before 1.6, to allow heart rate).
3. *Sync now*. Zepp passes data on every so often, and right away when you open it.

What comes through: steps (today and the week's average), last night's sleep, resting heart rate (or the lowest heart rate of the day when Zepp doesn't record a resting value) and workouts you record on the watch. They show on Today and in Profile → Smartwatch, count toward weekly active minutes, preselect the sleep answer in the pre-session check-in, and go to the coach. PoKreni recognises the watch by the app that wrote each record (`com.huami.watch.hmwatchmanager`), so steps from the phone or another app still show, just without the watch label. *Open Zepp* opens the app (or its Play Store page).

**Counted once.** A walk you log in PoKreni and the same walk recorded by the watch count once. An outside workout is treated as a repeat when it's on the same day as something logged here and either covers mostly the same time, or is of similar length (within 40% or 15 minutes) and the same kind of movement (a walk logged by hand later in the day), or of similar length within 3 hours when the kind is unknown. A watch workout during a PoKreni session is part of that session, and two apps recording the same workout count once. Tap one (on Today, or in Profile → Smartwatch, which lists the last 14 days with *Counted*, *Already logged* or *Hidden*) to give it your own name, hide it, or say *It's a different workout*. This only changes PoKreni; the record stays in Zepp and Health Connect. Workout types come from Health Connect (Walk, Run, Strength training…).

The rest timer already sends a notification when your rest ends; turn on PoKreni in the watch's app notifications in Zepp and the watch buzzes too.

## Exercise animations and videos

Each exercise sheet has up to four views:

- **Your clip:** a video (or GIF) you record at the gym or pick from your gallery. It's kept in the app's own storage on this phone (IndexedDB), plays offline, and comes first whenever there is one. Clips aren't part of backups.
- **Animation:** a figure drawn by the app (shaped limbs, hands, feet, and an eye to show which way the head faces), animated between key poses. Works offline. The parts that move are drawn in blue and the rest in navy (light figure in dark mode), worked out from how far each segment and the joint driving it turn over the cycle (`spec.still` keeps ride-along arms navy, `spec.work` forces a part blue); the joint that travels furthest leaves a dotted path (the dumbbell in a curl, the hips in a box squat). `tests/moves.test.js` checks every drawing at 48 moments: knees, elbows, hips and ankles only bend the way they can, nothing goes through the floor or out of the frame, nothing jumps, and hands and feet reach their handles, plates and floor spots.
- **Photos:** the start and end photo, alternating.
- **Video:** a short YouTube demo played inside the app, from established sources where possible (NASM, Gold's Gym, GoodLife Fitness, OPEX, Concept2). *Use a different video* takes any YouTube link; *Open on YouTube* is the fallback. The player loads only when you tap play and has no fullscreen button (the Android WebView doesn't do fullscreen video); it's removed when the sheet closes or the app goes to the background, and *Open on YouTube* hands the link to Android. Before 1.9 the player's own page history swallowed the Back button, so the sheet seemed frozen after visiting YouTube.

Real footage that may be shipped inside the app doesn't exist for most of these exercises, which is why bundled demos are drawn and real video is either yours or streamed.

Each warm-up move has an ⓘ button too: an animation, why it helps, how to do it, and a link to the full guide of the matching exercise (videos and your clip).

*Ask the coach* on an exercise starts the question for you: how to do it and what weight to use (or how many reps, how long to hold it, how long and hard to go). The coach gets that exercise's full history and the app's next target, or is told it's new to you.

## Adding exercises yourself

*Add exercise* on Today (or in the exercise sheet, or during a session) lets you pick any exercise from the library, by area or by typing in the search box (English or Croatian name, muscle, equipment; accents optional, so *cucanj* finds *Čučanj*), and choose **Just today** or **Every** session of that A/B day. Added ones are tagged and can be removed with ✕. During a session it goes straight into the session, optionally kept for next time. **Remove** (in the open exercise's tools) takes any exercise out of the session you're doing, with Undo; if you've logged sets of it, or it's one you keep in every session of that A/B day, it asks first and offers to stop adding it. Injuries still apply: exercises an injury rules out can't be picked.

The exercise library at the bottom of Learn has the same search.

## Body map, injuries and your own workouts

Progress opens with a front and back figure. Each muscle is coloured by its sets per week (last 7 days or 4 weeks) on one warm scale, grey for none, then under 3, 3+, 6+ and 10+ (the same lines as the ranks), from orange to deep red in light mode and from dim brick to bright coral in dark mode, so more sets always stands out more (the ramp is checked for even lightness steps and contrast against the figure): a set counts fully for the exercise's main muscles and half for supporting ones. Walks and rides aren't counted.

- **Ranks** need both: enough weeks in a row, and enough sets in each of them. Started 1 week of 3+ sets, Bronze 2 weeks of 3+, Silver 4 weeks of 6+, Gold 8 weeks of 10+, Diamond 16 weeks of 10+ (about 10 sets a week is a common target for building muscle). One big week can't skip a rank, and a muscle that only just gets 3 sets a week stays Bronze. Tapping a muscle shows the ladder: what each rank needs and how many weeks you have toward the next. The week in progress doesn't break a run until it's over.
- **Injuries** are marked by tapping a muscle or joint (or Profile → Injuries), with a side, and get a warning triangle on the figure (yellow for ease off, red for avoid); the colour itself only ever means sets. *Ease off* keeps the exercises that load it, with one set fewer and about 20% less weight, without changing your stored numbers. *Avoid* swaps each exercise that loads it for one that trains the same muscles without it, or leaves it out; warm-up moves that load it are hidden. Marking pain 6+/10 in a session offers to mark it.
- **Least trained**: once there are 2 sessions in 4 weeks, the card names the muscles with the least work. A **focus block** (2–4 weeks) adds one exercise for a muscle to every A/B session, then stops.
- **Other workout** on Today: build one for an area (back, legs, chest…), use a saved one, or go back to the programme. Built workouts use exercises you know first, never one your injuries rule out, and your own weights. They don't move the A/B queue.

The coach gets the same picture: sets per muscle, streaks, least-trained muscles, injuries with your notes and any focus block. Asked to build a workout, it picks exercises by id and the app checks them: unknown, repeated or injury-blocked ones are dropped, sets kept to 1–4, and weights and reps come from your history. If its answer is broken, the app builds one itself from the muscle group you named. It can also suggest a focus block, which you start with one tap.

## Ask the coach

The coach answers questions about training, pain, recovery and food, using your recent sessions, the plan, the lessons you've done and what Tanjur knows (weight, targets, recent meals). It answers in the language of your question and links to the matching lessons and exercises.

- **No keys of its own.** In Tanjur, Profile → AI, switch on *Let PoKreni use these AI keys*. Tanjur then passes its Claude, OpenRouter and Groq keys to PoKreni over the direct link. PoKreni keeps them in memory only, never in its data file or backups, and forgets them if you switch it off.
- **One Claude limit.** Both apps report what they spent this month, and Tanjur's monthly Claude limit covers the total. Once it's reached, the free models answer until next month.
- **Safety first.** Questions that sound like an emergency (chest pain, fainting, numbness, and the like, in English or Croatian) show the 112 card before any AI answer. The coach is told never to diagnose or suggest medicines, and to send you to a doctor when pain or symptoms call for it.
- **Your notes.** Profile → *For the coach* is a short free-text field (injuries, what your doctor said, how flexible you are) that's added to every question. The app itself assumes nothing about your body, job or schedule; the coach is told to rely only on these notes.
- **Own tab.** The coach has its own tab in the bottom bar; Profile is the round button at the top right.
- **Memory.** Nothing is remembered on its own. Under a chat, *Save to memory* has the AI summarise the part not saved yet in 1–4 points (goals, decisions, pains mentioned, the advice and its numbers); you can edit the note before keeping it, and it goes to the top of *Coach's memory* above the chat. *Delete chat* clears the chat (it warns if part of it isn't saved). The notes are in your order of importance: drag one by its handle (or use the arrow keys on it) to move it, and the coach gets them from the top down until about 2,400 characters; notes below that are greyed out as not used. Tap a note to edit it, use the bin to delete it (with Undo), or *Forget everything*. *Use memory in answers* switches it off without deleting anything. Up to 40 notes, kept in PoKreni's data (and backups).

Your question, the last few messages and that summary go to the AI service; nothing else leaves the phone.

## Build it on your PC instead

Needs Node 22+, JDK 21 and Android Studio.

```bash
npm install
npm test
npx cap sync android
npx cap open android
```

To try the app in a desktop browser, serve `www/` (for example `python -m http.server` inside it) and open `index.html`. Health Connect, notifications and opening Tanjur only work in the Android app.

## Safety

PoKreni gives general guidance for healthy beginners, not medical advice. The readiness check is based on the PAR-Q; the official, longer PAR-Q+ is at eparmedx.com.
