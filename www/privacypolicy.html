PoKreni privacy policy

PoKreni is a personal training app. It has no account and no server of its own.

Health data

If you connect Health Connect, PoKreni writes your finished training sessions and logged walks or rides (type, start and end time, title, estimated active calories) so other apps you allow, such as Tanjur and Samsung Health, can use them. It reads your body weight, last night's sleep, steps, workouts recorded by other apps and the nutrition logged in Tanjur, only to estimate calories, count your weekly active minutes and show today's protein. PoKreni never sends health data off your phone.

Where your data is stored

Your training log, lessons, tests and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you control.

Removing access

You can remove PoKreni's access in Android Settings, Health Connect, App permissions.
